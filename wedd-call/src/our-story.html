Stacey & Peter

Our Story

Seven years, two cities, one very slow proposal. This is how we got from a spilled coffee in a university library to a church in December — the long version, for anyone who has only heard it at a party.

How It Happened

Chapter By Chapter

The first meet — September 2015

Stacey was working the late desk at the university library and Peter was the student who knocked a full cup of coffee across her returns trolley at ten past nine on a Tuesday. He came back the next evening to apologise, and the evening after that with no excuse at all.

She says she knew by the third Tuesday. He says the fifth. They have agreed to disagree.

Read the post

First date — October 2015

A Vietnamese place on Chapel Street that has since closed, which they both take personally. It rained, the bus did not come, and they walked forty minutes back to campus sharing one umbrella and an argument about films that has never really finished.

Peter still has the receipt. Stacey found it in a book two years later and has never let him forget it.

The proposal — August 2017

The cliff path above Sunset Beach, a Thursday, no photographer and no plan beyond a ring he had carried for three weeks looking for a moment that was not raining. She said yes before he had finished the question; the ring was a size too big and stayed on with a plaster until Monday.

The photograph on this page was taken by a passing family who asked what they had just watched.

The wedding day

Our Wedding — December

St. Thomas's, because it is where Peter's parents married and because it has a roof, which in December matters. The reception is at the hall down the lane so nobody has to drive between them. Everything we know so far is on the events page; the timetable for the day is on the travel page.

We would rather see you there than receive a present. Truly.
