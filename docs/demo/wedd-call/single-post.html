We're getting married!!

August 20 2017 Wedding

It happened on a Thursday, on the cliff path above Sunset Beach, with no photographer, no plan and a ring that had been in Peter's jacket pocket for three weeks waiting for a day that was not raining. Stacey said yes before he had finished the question. The ring was a size too big and stayed on with a plaster until the jeweller opened on Monday.

So: we are getting married. The date is the 25th of December — yes, Christmas Day, and yes, we have thought about it. Peter's parents married at St. Thomas's on Christmas Eve thirty-one years ago and the church offered us the day after; the vicar says it has been done twice before and both couples are still married, which we are taking as a sign.

The plan, as far as it goes: the ceremony at four, drinks in the vicarage garden if the weather holds and in the vicarage if it does not, dinner and dancing at the village hall down the lane until midnight. Everything we know is on the events page, and the timetable, the trains and where to sleep are on the travel page. The RSVP form is open now; we would love an answer by the first of December so the hall knows how many chairs to find.

We will write here now and then in the run-up — the dress (nothing), the cake (opinions), the shoes (a whole post, apparently). Subscribe to nothing; just check back. And if you have a memory of the two of us you would like us to have on the day, the wishes page is where it goes.
