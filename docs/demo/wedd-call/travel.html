Getting Here

By Road, Rail And Air

By car

From the motorway take junction 12 and follow the signs to the village for eleven miles; the church is on the left as you come in, the hall's car park is behind it and holds sixty. There is no parking on the lane itself, and the neighbours will notice.

Postcode for the satnav: use the hall's, not the church's — the church's takes you to a farm.

View On Map

By train

Trains from the city run every half hour to Millbrook, which is fifteen minutes from the church by taxi. We have booked two taxis to meet the 2:40 and the 3:10 arrivals; tell us on the RSVP if you would like a seat in one.

The last train back is at 11:52, which is before the dancing ends. Most people are staying over.

Book a seat

By air

The nearest airport is an hour and ten minutes by car, or a train to the city and on to Millbrook. Hire cars are cheaper booked from home than at the desk; the village has no petrol station, so fill up on the way.

If you are flying in the day before, the Millbrook hotels below are the closest beds to the station.

Where to stay

Where To Stay

Beds Nearby

The Crown, Millbrook

Hotel — 15 minutes

Twelve rooms above the pub by the station, and the one most of the wedding party is in. Quote 'Stacey and Peter' for the wedding rate; breakfast is until eleven on the Sunday, which is deliberate.

The Old Rectory

Bed & breakfast — 2 minutes

Four rooms next to the church. Walking distance to everything, including the dancing; booked by the families, but ask, as one room may come free.

Hillside Farm

Self-catering — 8 minutes

Three cottages sleeping four to six, ideal for a family or a group coming for the weekend. Dogs welcome, which at the church they are not.

Millbrook Lodge

Motel — 12 minutes

Cheapest and closest to the motorway. Nothing to write home about, clean, and forty pounds a night.
